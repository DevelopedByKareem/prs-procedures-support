PRS Procedures

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open PRS Procedures and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm the deletion and, if asked, re-enter your password.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile information (name, email address, university, level of training)
  • Your quiz scores, answers, and attempt history
  • Your sign-in account

This cannot be undone. We do not keep a copy of this data after deletion.

Can't access the app?

Email us from the address registered to your account at support@prsprocedures.com with the subject "Delete my account", and we will delete your account and data.
