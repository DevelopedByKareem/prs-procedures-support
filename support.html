About the App

PRS Procedures is an educational app for medical students, residents, and attending physicians in Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery. Browse surgical procedures organized by body system, test your knowledge with interactive quizzes, and track your progress — available in English and French.

Common Questions

I can't log in.
Make sure you've verified your email address. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for the verification email. You can request a new one from the login screen.

How do I reset my password?
Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen and enter your email. You'll receive a link to reset your password.

How do I switch languages?
Open the drawer menu (hamburger icon in the top left) and tap the language toggle to switch between English and French.

How do I delete my account?
Open the menu, go to Profile, and tap "Delete Account" at the bottom. See how to delete your account for details.
